In ragam mode, s/r/g/m/p/d/n resolve using the current ragam. In swara mode they resolve using a default ragam unless you give a variant like r1, g2, n3.
Notes run together play consecutively (pmg = P, M, G); a comma repeats the previous note; digits (r1, g2) pick a variant; ' / . mark upper/lower octave, same as elsewhere. Ḍ/Ṇ and Ṡ/Ṙ/Ġ/Ṁ (dot-below/dot-above, as printed in notation sheets) also work directly as lower/upper octave. Lines starting with # are comments and are skipped — use them for section labels. Bars (|), spaces and anything else are ignored. Paste only the swaram line(s) — not lyrics or headers. Plays using BPM × Notes/beat; check "Fast notes" to make lowercase letters play at half the duration of uppercase.
Number/letter keys = play notes  |  ↑/↓ = sruthi  |  Tab = mode  |  PgUp/PgDn = ragam  |  Enter = arohanam  |  Backspace = avarohanam  |  Green = in ragam  |  Gold = just played  |  Group colors: S/P gold, R blue, G teal, M red, D purple, N pink
Metronome and Shruti Box run independently of Swara/Ragam mode and keep going until toggled off. Arohanam/Avarohanam and typed swara sequences play in tempo with the BPM setting. Veena and Tanpura are synthesized plucked strings (no free sample library exists for them), not recordings.
